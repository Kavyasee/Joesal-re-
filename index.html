🏏 Indian Cricket Superstars

1. Shubman Gill

Shubman Gill is an Indian international cricketer known for his elegant batting style, excellent timing and consistency. He is one of India's leading young batting talents.

2. Virat Kohli

Virat Kohli is one of India's most celebrated cricketers. He is famous for his aggressive batting, fitness, consistency and determination.

3. Suryakumar Yadav

Suryakumar Yadav is famous for his innovative batting and amazing 360-degree shots. He is popularly known as SKY.

4. Ravindra Jadeja

Ravindra Jadeja is an outstanding all-rounder. He is known for powerful batting, accurate bowling and exceptional fielding.

5. MS Dhoni

Mahendra Singh Dhoni is a legendary former Indian captain and wicketkeeper-batter. He led India to major international victories and is admired for his calm leadership.
